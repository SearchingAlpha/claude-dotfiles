# Pitch card — 3:00. Print it, keep it by the laptop.

## Skeleton (over time? cut "how it works" first; never cut the demo)

| Time | Slot | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Problem + who | Persona, pain, one number. Their vocabulary. |
| 0:30–0:45 | Why now | One sentence: what changed. |
| 0:45–1:00 | What we built | "Así que construimos [nombre]: un [tipo] que [hace Y] para [quién]." |
| 1:00–2:15 | **DEMO** | Narrate where to look. Aha moment by 1:40. Dev drives, you talk. |
| 2:15–2:35 | How it works + what's real | Architecture in one breath. Say what's mocked. |
| 2:35–2:50 | Impact + why us | The number and the formula's gist. Team edge in one line. |
| 2:50–3:00 | Close | Next step: pilot, or what you'd build next. One memorable line. |

## Delivery

First and last sentence memorised. Slow down on the number. Pause after the
aha. Eyes on the panel, not the screen. Spanish, short sentences.

## Fallback ladder

Live → cached → video (tab 2) → screenshots. Count to three; if nothing:
**"Os enseño la ejecución grabada."** Switch and keep talking. Never debug on
stage.

## Q&A: four steps, out loud

1. **Reframe.** "Si entiendo bien, preguntas si X para Y. Empiezo por Z."
2. **Structure.** "Lo veo en tres partes: A, B, C."
3. **Prioritise and assume.** "Empezaría por A porque… Asumo que…; si no,
   cambiaría a B."
4. **Close.** "Así que el primer paso sería X, y lo validaría con Y."

Blank silence is the only failure. Narrate. Anchor in your domain (supply
chain, forecasting, ops). You may ask a question back.

## Ten likely questions (15-second answers ready)

1. How big is this, who pays? 2. Why wouldn't [company] build it? 3. What's
real vs mocked? 4. How does it handle [the messy case]? 5. How does it
integrate with [company]'s product? 6. Failure mode, and how would a user
notice? 7. Which model, why, cost and latency at scale? 8. What's defensible?
9. What would you do with the €5k or the next three months? 10. Why this team?
