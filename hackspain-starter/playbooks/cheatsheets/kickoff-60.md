# Kickoff 60 — the first hour at the venue

Facilitator card. Run it cold. You facilitate, you don't decide alone.

**The first two minutes, say this:**

> "Quick proposal so we're coding by hour one: 5 minutes intros and who's
> strong at what; 10 minutes each of us reads the challenges and writes one
> line; 15 minutes of ideas with no debate; 10 minutes to score and vote;
> 10 to lock scope and roles; 10 for the CLI and a first commit. I'll take
> product, the business case, the pitch and the demo, and I'll keep us honest
> on scope. You own the stack and the code. Okay?"

| Min | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Intros: name, what you're best at, what you want from the weekend. Map who is frontend, backend, LLM/agents, data, infra. Pick the demo-laptop owner. | Skill map |
| 5–15 | Read all five challenges in silence. Each writes, for the one or two they like: "[Co] wants [who] to [outcome] without [pain]." | One-liners |
| 15–30 | Diverge: five or more ideas, one line each, no "how", no debate. Prompts: obvious automation · control tower · exception handler · copilot · data product · our-domain wedge · contrarian. | Idea list |
| 30–40 | Score 1–5: Impact · Demoable in 30h · Track fit · Team fit. Kill anything at 2 or below on Demoable. Dot-vote the top two. You propose, the team confirms. | Chosen idea |
| 40–50 | Lock: one-liner · demo moment ("judge does X, sees Y in N seconds, today takes Z") · tracer bullet (input → step → output, what's faked) · NOT-doing list of five · roles. Paste the Framing Brief in chat and pin it. | Framing Brief |
| 50–60 | `hackspain team create <name>` and share the join code · `track register <slug>` · `team repo <url>` · `stack set …` · `submit --draft` with name and two-line description · first commit · `milestone add firstCommit` · `watch` running on one machine. | Team on the board |

**Stuck at minute 40?** Take the idea with the highest Demoable score. Speed
beats optimal. You can re-scope at H3 with a mentor.

**Your job right after H1:** write the problem paragraph and the impact
formula while the devs scaffold. Run `/hs-frame` with the challenge text to
pressure-test the brief. At H2 run `hackspain project list` for duplicate
ideas in your track. Book a mentor for H3–H4.

**Watch for:** an idea chosen because it's technically fun · a demo moment
nobody can say in one sentence · two people on the same task · nobody owning
the demo data · a second track that needs extra work.
